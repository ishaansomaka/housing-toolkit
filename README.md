# Atlanta Region Housing Pressure Toolkit

An interactive, bilingual (English/Spanish) website that tracks early warning signs of housing pressure and displacement across the 11-county Atlanta region.

**Live site:** https://ishaansomaka.github.io/housing-toolkit/

## What it does
- Maps and compares 14 housing and economic indicators for Cherokee, Clayton, Cobb, DeKalb, Douglas, Fayette, Forsyth, Fulton, Gwinnett, Henry, and Rockdale counties
- Flags seven transparent early warning signals for each county, with the rule behind every signal
- Shows 2010 to 2024 change with Census significance tests, and adjusts incomes for inflation
- Compares each county's share of today's homes with its share of forecast growth to 2035
- Lets residents report changes they see in their neighborhoods

## Data sources
- U.S. Census Bureau, American Community Survey 5-year estimates (2006-2010 and 2020-2024), compiled by the Atlanta Regional Commission Open Data & Mapping Hub
- Atlanta Regional Commission Series 17 household forecasts (ARC Board meeting packet, March 11, 2026)
- U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics CPI-U annual averages, for inflation adjustment
- U.S. Census Bureau cartographic boundary files
